## 1. Purpose

L➿P_BLR_by__⩜⃝☠️__777 is designed to manage weekly staff rosters while preserving operational rules, weekly offs, shift rotation, Royal Shift requirements and Break Duty requirements.

---

# 2. Core Assignment Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1ST | First Shift |
| 2ND | Second Shift |
| RL | Royal Shift, opening to closing |
| BRK | Break Duty |
| WO | Weekly Off |

---

# 3. Important Rule

Do NOT treat all five codes as ordinary shifts.

Only 1ST and 2ND rotate.

RL, BRK and WO are special assignments.

---

# 4. Weekly Roster Workflow

### Step 1
Open the roster system.

### Step 2
Select the week to generate.

### Step 3
The system preserves existing weekly-off patterns.

### Step 4
The system generates normal 1ST/2ND rotation.

### Step 5
The system checks minimum staffing requirements.

### Step 6
If normal staffing is insufficient, the system identifies eligible RL candidates.

### Step 7
RL candidates are presented for administrator approval.

### Step 8
Approved RL assignments become part of the final roster.

### Step 9
BRK duties can be created according to operational demand.

### Step 10
The system validates the roster before publication.

---

# 5. Royal Shift

RL means:

**Opening → Closing**

Rules:

- Any role except GM and OM may be eligible.
- Maximum one RL per person per week.
- RL may be assigned even when the person's normal rotation is 1ST or 2ND.
- RL counts toward both shift coverage requirements.
- RL requires administrator approval.

---

# 6. Break Duty

BRK is operational break coverage.

BRK:

- Can be added according to daily demand.
- Can be updated during operation.
- Applies to a specific duty period.
- Does not change normal 1ST/2ND rotation.
- Requires appropriate approval.

---

# 7. Weekly Off

WO means Weekly Off.

The system should preserve the employee's normal weekly-off day.

A weekly-off change must be treated as an authorized manual override and recorded in the audit trail.

---

# 8. Administrator Responsibilities

Administrators should:

- Review generated rosters.
- Review RL proposals.
- Approve or reject special assignments.
- Review BRK requirements.
- Resolve validation errors.
- Approve authorized roster changes.
- Ensure the final roster is published only after validation.

---

# 9. Staff Responsibilities

Staff should:

- Follow the published roster.
- Report according to the assigned reporting group.
- Follow 1ST, 2ND, RL and BRK assignments exactly.
- Observe their assigned Weekly Off.
- Never change assignments without authorization.

---

# 10. Reporting

The system groups reporting as follows:

### FIRST_SHIFT reporting

Includes:

- 1ST
- RL
- BRK

### SECOND_SHIFT reporting

Includes:

- 2ND

### NO reporting

- WO

Actual reporting times are controlled by roster configuration.

---

# 11. What Users Should NOT Do

Do not:

- Manually alter a generated roster without authorization.
- Treat WO as a shift.
- Count BRK as a rotation step.
- Count RL as a normal rotation step.
- Assign more than one RL to the same person in one week.
- Publish a roster with unresolved critical validation errors.

---

# 12. Administrator Approval

Special assignments that alter the generated operational plan must go through the approval workflow.

The system should preserve:

- Who made the change
- What was changed
- When it was changed
- Why it was changed
- Previous assignment
- New assignment
